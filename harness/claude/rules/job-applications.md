# Applying to jobs on the person's behalf

- Country: United States.
- Location: Jersey City, New Jersey or Dallas, Texas, whichever is closer to
  the job listing. Use the resume that belongs to that location and portal.
- Fill every field on the form, not just the required ones.
- When a question is unclear, find the answer in `~/StudioProjects/omar-iqbal`
  (the portfolio site: `src/data/experience.ts`, `src/data/approach.ts`,
  `src/data/opensource.ts`, the projects under `src/content/projects/`, and
  `public/Omar_Iqbal_Resume.pdf`). The portal's `data/profile.json` is the
  first source; the portfolio is the second.
- Never invent an answer. If neither source answers a required question, stop
  that application and report the question instead of guessing.
- Pace LinkedIn applications at one every 5 to 10 minutes, and stop the whole
  run on any security check.
- If the same listing is opened more than twice, stop trying it: move it to
  the "needs your help" list (`needs_review`) and never auto-apply to it.
  A listing with no Easy Apply button is external; leave it in needs_review
  too, since the tool cannot fill it from a LinkedIn link.
